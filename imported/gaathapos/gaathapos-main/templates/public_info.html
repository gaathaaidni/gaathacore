Privacy Policy

Information used to run restaurant operations.

Last Updated: September 21, 2026

Gaatha POS can process account details, restaurant and menu records, orders, payments, inventory activity, audit activity, uploaded imports, and technical information needed to operate the application.

This information is used to authenticate users, provide authorized workflows, maintain records, protect the application, and support operational reporting. The application may also use configured email, payment, tax, exchange-rate, or monitoring integrations when enabled by an operator.

Specific retention periods, recipients, jurisdiction, and deletion processes are not established by the repository and are intentionally not stated here. Ask your organization administrator for the applicable operational details.
