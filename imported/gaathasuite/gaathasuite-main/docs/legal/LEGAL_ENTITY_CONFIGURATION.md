# Legal Entity Configuration

**Status:** REQUIRES BUSINESS CONFIRMATION and REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW.

Gaatha Suite must identify one primary contracting and operating legal entity for customer agreements, subscriptions, invoices, privacy obligations, and legal notices. The repository must not arbitrarily declare Gaatha Ventures Sh.P.K. or Aidni Global LLP to be that entity.

The second entity may be identified as a technology, development, or operations provider only if that relationship is accurate and approved by the business owner and counsel.
