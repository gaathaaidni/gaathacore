## Interpretation

The competitive gap is not that Gaatha lacks a roadmap. It lacks the same breadth and hard verification of mature ERP suites. The right strategic posture is to avoid trying to mimic every module and instead win with a coherent product architecture, strong AI orientation, and simple SME workflows.
