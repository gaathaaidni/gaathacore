# GAATHA SUITE ROADMAP

## NOW

Priority items required for the next release.

- Harden the Enterprise Foundation
  - organization hierarchy and configuration
  - RBAC enforcement and permission matrix
  - multitenant route isolation across create, read, update, delete, export, and search
  - audit logging for auth and critical business actions
- Verify auth and org onboarding flows end-to-end
- Add cross-tenant regression tests for protected API routes
- Complete the production-safe migration and health checks
- Finish security review of exports, uploads, and downloads

## NEXT

High-value modules that depend on the foundation.

- CRM lead-to-customer workflow
- Opportunity pipeline and sales activity tracking
- Quotation and sales order lifecycle
- Basic accounting core and invoice flow
- Expense and approval rules
- Procurement and vendor record management
- Inventory stock and movement integration

## LATER

Important but not the immediate requirement set.

- Advanced accounting cycles and close controls
- Projects, timesheets, and resource planning
- HR employee lifecycle and attendance/leave tracking
- Helpdesk and customer support workflows
- More advanced reporting and dashboards
- Internal portal experience improvements

## FUTURE

Longer runway, ecosystem-level features.

- AI business insight and agent-driven ops
- Automations using WHEN/IF/THEN logic
- Connected integrations and API platform
- Customer and vendor portals
- POS/eCommerce and manufacturing
- Globalized tax/jurisdiction model
- Marketplace, extensions, and ecosystem tools

## Strategic note

The roadmap intentionally avoids putting every phase into NOW. The product should evolve from a secure foundation into integrated operations, not from a generic feature list into a fragile platform.
