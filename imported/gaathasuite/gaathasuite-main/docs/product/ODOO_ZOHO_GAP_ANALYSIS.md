# Odoo / Zoho Gap Analysis

## Capability grid

| Capability | Gaatha status | Odoo / Zoho benchmark | Gap | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CRM | Partially implemented in source; workflow verification incomplete | Mature CRM and lead-to-sale lifecycle | Medium | High |
| Sales | Partially represented; quotation-to-invoice flow not fully proven | Mature sales stack | High | High |
| Accounting | Basic models exist; financial integrity and close controls not verified | Mature accounting and ledger controls | High | Critical |
| Inventory | Model primitives present; movement and valuation not fully proven | Mature stock and warehouse management | High | High |
| Projects | Source presence but limited verified workflow maturity | Strong project management capabilities | Medium | Medium |
| HR | Department and employee structures exist; lifecycle not fully verified | Mature HR and payroll coverage | Medium | Medium |
| Helpdesk | Not proven as full production workflow | Strong support infrastructure | High | Medium |
| Automation | Framework direction exists; business rules not verified | Mature workflow automation | High | High |
| Analytics | Reporting structure exists but not full BI layer | Mature analytics and dashboards | Medium | High |
| AI | Partial AI assistant surfaces; business insight layer not complete | AI-native suggestions and executive insight | High | Critical |
| Integrations | Partial framework and vendor routes; not broad integration verified | Mature ecosystem integrations | Medium | High |
| India support | Country-aware architecture is a future direction | Localized compliance and tax features | High | Medium |
| Internationalization | Architecture is conceptually global-ready, not yet proven | Mature multi-country support | High | Medium |
| UX | Foundation exists; consistent product UX still evolving | Mature SaaS UX patterns | Medium | Medium |
| Security | Strong baseline but incomplete proof across all routes | Mature enterprise security controls | High | Critical |
| API | Basic API platform present, but not complete or fully versioned | Mature API-first platform | Medium | High |

## Summary

The largest gaps remain in business-process completion and enterprise-grade governance rather than in basic app scaffolding. In other words, Gaatha is not missing only features; it is missing end-to-end trust in cross-tenant security, financial integrity, and workflow completeness.
