TERMS AND CONDITIONS

Use publishing tools responsibly.

Use PostPilot only for lawful content operations and only with the authorization required by your deployment and connected provider accounts. Review content, targets, and media before publishing.

PRIVACY POLICY

Information used to operate content workflows.

Last Updated: September 21, 2026

PostPilot can process post text, post categories, media files, posting status, configured intervals, logs, and technical information needed to run the application. When configured, it also interacts with external social or content providers using credentials supplied by the operator.

Information is used to store and manage content, process uploads, operate configured publishing tasks, and maintain the application. The repository does not establish retention periods, recipients, jurisdiction, or deletion procedures, so those details are intentionally not stated here.

USER POLICY

Responsible use for publishing teams.

Last Updated: September 21, 2026

Keep deployment and provider credentials secure. Publish only content you are authorized to use, respect platform rules and audience rights, and verify posts before enabling automated workflows.
